# ai-happy-01 — the happiest image

A laughing toddler and a golden retriever puppy tumbling together in a pile
of golden autumn leaves at golden hour: the puppy licks the toddler's cheek
while the toddler laughs with eyes squeezed shut, leaves scattering in warm
sunlight all around them.

One look, one mood: pure, unguarded delight. No text, no clutter — just two
big happy faces and warm amber light that reads clearly even at 300 pixels.

`main.py` copies the repository's `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
Deterministic, standard library only, no network.
